The words PTA and A.I. are typically not said in the same sentence. I'm serving as PTA Treasurer on my child's school PTA. I served as president last year.  (Fun fact, the [National PTA](https://www.pta.org/home/About-National-Parent-Teacher-Association) is the oldest and largest child advocacy organization in the United States)

Being treasurer involves a lot of paperwork includ9ing writing checks, documenting reimbursements and deposits. I was thinking of ways I could leverage AI in helping me with some of these tasks and learn about how to write AI agents. 

I started a GitHub repo [pta-treasurer-agent](https://github.com/indapa/pta-treasurer-agent)
that has two agents:

**Reimbursement Agent** that checks that receipts support the amount claimed on a reimbursement form. This can be a cumbersome process becasue volunteers can submit many receipts on a reimbursment, and it was tedious to look through all of them to make sure the added up to the correct amount. 

**Cash Verification Agent** checks to make sure the amount on a cash verification form matches what is on a bank deposit slip. This agent is just a sanity check to make sure bank deposit slips and cash verification paper work matchup. 

Note, I am still checking the output becasue AI can misread a scan or handwriting. 
This was a good starter project to learn about [LangGraph](https://github.com/langchain-ai/langgraph) and make  my volunteer duties a little more efficient.
