As a former PTA President and current Treasurer, I handle a lot of paperwork—especially verifying reimbursement forms and matching cash deposit slips. To streamline this and learn LangGraph, I built [pta-treasurer-agent](https://github.com/indapa/pta-treasurer-agent).

The repository features two agents:

Reimbursement Agent: Verifies that multiple submitted receipts match the total claimed on reimbursement forms.

Cash Verification Agent: Runs a sanity check to ensure cash verification forms match bank deposit slips.

While I still double-check the results to catch any OCR or handwriting errors, this project makes my volunteer work more efficient while giving me hands-on experience building AI agents.
